# Part XVII — Aerospace

Aerospace uses lasers where materials are expensive, hard to machine and safety-critical: millions of cooling holes in turbine hot sections, titanium and superalloy welds, repair of high-value blades and blisks, additively manufactured engine parts, and surface treatments for fatigue life. Process qualification (and the cost of changing a qualified process) dominates technology choice.

| Material | Processing challenge | Laser approaches |
|---|---|---|
| Titanium alloys (Ti-6Al-4V) | Reactive with O and N when hot; embrittlement | Fiber welding and cutting with full inert shielding (trailing shields, chambers); LPBF and DED |
| Aluminium alloys (2xxx, 7xxx, Al-Li, 6xxx) | Hot cracking, porosity, high reflectivity | Fiber with filler wire and wobble; historical fuselage stringer-to-skin laser beam welding on 6xxx alloys |
| Nickel superalloys (IN718, IN625, single-crystal) | Strain-age and solidification cracking; hard to machine | Controlled-heat-input welding; drilling; cladding and DED repair; LPBF |
| Composites (CFRP, CMC) | Anisotropic, HAZ, fiber pull-out, delamination | ps/ns UV or high-power fiber for trimming and drilling with multi-pass heat control; ultrafast for CMCs |

| Application | Component | Laser (typical) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cutting | Ti and Ni sheet, CFRP panels, CMC parts | Fiber CW; ps/fs for composites and CMC | 3D 5-axis trimming |
| Welding | Ducts, casings, brackets, seat tracks, fuselage stringers | Fiber, disk | Low heat input, low distortion vs arc and EB welding |
| Drilling | Turbine blade, vane and combustor cooling holes (through thermal-barrier coatings) | ms QCW fiber or Nd:YAG (percussion, trepanning); ps for recast-free and shaped holes | Very high hole counts per engine; recast and micro-crack limits |
| Cladding | Blade tips, seals, wear surfaces | DED with fiber or diode | Controlled dilution |
| Repair | Blisk airfoils, compressor blades, casings | DED (powder or wire) with adaptive toolpaths | High value per repaired part |
| Additive manufacturing | Fuel nozzles, brackets, heat exchangers, rocket-engine chambers | Multi-laser LPBF (fiber); large-scale DED | Consolidates assemblies; requires powder and process qualification |
| Surface treatment | Fan and compressor blades; airframe surfaces | Laser shock peening (J-class ns); ns fiber cleaning and depainting; texturing for bonding | Fatigue life; chemical-free paint removal |
| Marking | Part UID and wire identification | Fiber (DPM), UV (wire insulation) | DPM quality standards for aerospace parts; UV wire marking |

Directed-energy weapons are laser products for defence, not manufacturing processes; they are outside this handbook’s scope except as a demand driver for high-power fiber and beam-combining technology.
