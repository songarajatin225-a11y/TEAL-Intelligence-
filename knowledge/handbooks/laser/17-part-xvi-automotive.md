# Part XVI — Automotive

The car is the largest single consumer of industrial laser kilowatts: 3D cutting of press-hardened steel, remote welding of closures, brazed roof seams, and — with electrification — hairpin stators, inverters and battery packs. Electronics, sensors and lighting add thousands of smaller marking, soldering and plastic-welding operations.

| System | Component | Material | Laser process | Laser type (typical) | Machine type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Body-in-white | B-pillars, sills, bumpers (press-hardened) | Boron steel with Al-Si coating | 3D trimming and hole cutting | 3–6 kW fiber | 5-axis 3D cutting cell |
| Body-in-white | Tailor-welded blanks | Different steel grades/gauges; Al-Si-coated blanks | Coating ablation at edges, butt welding | Pulsed fiber (ablation), multi-kW fiber/disk (welding) | Blank welding line |
| Body-in-white | Doors, closures, rear structures | Zinc-coated steel, Al | Remote scanner welding | 4–8 kW fiber or disk, scanner optics | Robot + remote welding head |
| Body-in-white | Roof and tailgate seams | Galvanised steel + CuSi₃ wire | Laser brazing | 2–4 kW diode/fiber, twin-spot or ring optics | Robot with tactile brazing head |
| Powertrain | Gears, differential housings, shafts | Case-hardened steels | Keyhole welding; hardening | Fiber/disk CW; diode | Rotary welding station |
| Powertrain | Fuel-injector spray holes | Hardened steel | Helical drilling | ps/fs | Precision drilling machine |
| Powertrain | Cylinder bores, piston rings | Cast iron, Al with liners | Surface texturing, hardening | ns/ps fiber; diode | Texturing and hardening cells |
| EV e-motor | Hairpin stator joints | Enamelled Cu | Insulation stripping; joint welding | Pulsed fiber or CO₂ (stripping); green, IR+blue or IR with scanner (welding), OCT monitoring | Hairpin welding station |
| EV e-motor | Rotor lamination stacks | Electrical steel | Seam welding; prototype lamination cutting | Fiber CW; single-mode fiber (cutting) | Welding fixture; fine cutter |
| EV inverter | Power-module terminals, busbars | Cu, Al | Welding | Green, IR fiber with shaping | Micro-welding cell |
| EV inverter | Ceramic substrates (DBC/AMB) | Al₂O₃, Si₃N₄, AlN with Cu | Scribing, singulation | CO₂, UV, ps | Substrate processing system |

| Sensors | Pressure, O₂, Hall, crash sensors | Stainless diaphragms, ceramics, PCBs | Hermetic welding, trimming, marking | QCW/pulsed fiber; trimming lasers; UV | Micro-welding, trimming |
| ADAS | Radar and camera housings | PBT, PC, PA | Transmission plastic welding | 9xx nm diode or fiber; Tm 2 µm for clear-to-clear | Contour or quasi-simultaneous welding |
