# Part XXI — Supplier Ecosystem

The laser industry is a five-tier value chain — materials and components, laser sources, subsystems, machines, and integration — with value and margin concentrated where technology is hardest to copy (diodes, ultrafast sources, scanners, process know-how) and price pressure strongest in commoditised CW and ns fiber sources. Since 2024 the Western incumbents have been reshaping portfolios while Chinese source makers have taken volume share. Company lists below are representative, not rankings; no market shares are asserted.

## 21.1 How the tiers make money

| Tier | Role | Where differentiation sits | Typical risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Materials and components | Diode chips, doped and passive fibers, nonlinear crystals, coated optics | Epitaxy, facet reliability, fiber design, coating LIDT | Capital-intensive, qualification-heavy |
| Laser sources | Oscillators and amplifiers sold to OEMs | Power scaling, beam quality, pulse control, reliability, cost per W | Price erosion in commoditised classes |
| Subsystems | Scanners, process heads, controllers, sensors, vision | Dynamics, calibration, software, integration | Dependence on source and OEM cycles |
| Machine OEMs | Complete machines for a process or industry | Application know-how, automation, software, service network | Capex cyclicality of end markets |
| System integrators and application specialists | Line integration, process development | Customer intimacy, domain processes | Project risk, scale |

Spectra-Physics, Ophir and Newport operate as brands of MKS; Lumentum continues to supply industrial ultrafast and DPSS sources alongside its optical-networking business (MKS; Lumentum).
