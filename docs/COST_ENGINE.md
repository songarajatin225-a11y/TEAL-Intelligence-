# Cost engine

Code: `src/calculations/cost.ts` (pure functions), `src/hooks/useCost.ts` (FX + approval bands
from data), `src/features/cost/` (UI). The roll-up is the legacy **TEAL Cost Platform**
`computeProject()` / `tealSheet()` logic, ported and proven identical on the platform's three
templates (`tests/unit/cost.test.ts` against `tests/fixtures/legacy-golden.json`, generated by
executing the untouched legacy JavaScript).

## 1. Cost modules (line grids)

| Key | Label | Line amount (legacy `MODULES[].calc`) |
|---|---|---|
| `material` | Material (landed BOM) | qty × unit price × FX |
| `mechanical` | Mechanical | qty × rate |
| `electrical` | Electrical | qty × unit price |
| `software` | Software & controls | man-days × rate |
| `manufacturing` | Manufacturing (machine-hour) | (setup h + run h) × rate |
| `labour` | Labour | man-days × rate + OT h × OT rate |
| `design` | Design & engineering (incl. NRE) | man-days × rate + NRE |
| `site` | Site & installation | persons × days × rate (+ site contingency) |
| `commercial` | Commercial | lump sum or % of the preceding direct cost |
| `commissioning` | Commissioning | hours × rate |
| `packaging` | Packing & crating | 2(LW + LH + WH) × boxes × rate/m² |
| `amc` | AMC (quoted separately) | Σ visits × cost × (1 + esc)^year |

A foreign-currency line without an FX rate is **excluded with a warning**, never converted at a
guessed rate. FX rates carry an `as_of` date; undated rates are flagged by data quality.

## 2. Roll-up (legacy `computeProject`)

```
basic      = material bucket
material   = basic × (1 + freight% + duty% + landing%)     GST computed and shown, not added to cost
site       = site × (1 + site contingency%)
commercial = computed on the direct base of the other equipment buckets
direct     = Σ equipment buckets (everything except AMC)
overheads  = direct × overhead%
contingency= (direct + overheads) × contingency%
total cost = direct + overheads + contingency
profit     = total cost × profit%
selling    = total cost + profit
margins    = gross / net on selling; per-unit and order value by qty
```

## 3. TEAL cost sheet (A / B / C / D)

`A` = raw material + bought-outs + manufactured parts + packing & forwarding + insurance +
boarding; `B` = assembly + installation + debug/OQC + SG&A + OSS; `C = A + B`;
**`D = C / (1 − profit% − warranty%)`** (gross-up; Automation Handbook Part 54 formula C1).
Defaults: `TEAL_DEFAULTS` (insurance 0.05 %, SG&A 8 %, warranty 2.5 %, profit 12 %, OSS 65 000/month
× 0.5 HC × 12 months, packing 1 600/m²) — overridable per cost model.

## 4. Decision support

| Function | What it answers |
|---|---|
| `approvalFloor` | minimum margin for the order-value band (approval bands from `data/cost/rates.json`) |
| `priceToWin` | mark-up needed to hit a target price; whether it clears the approval floor |
| `landedCost` | imported item: base + freight + duty + insurance + clearing, keeping original currency, FX, date and source (Handbook C4) |
| `priceFromMargin` | price for a target margin, grossed up (Handbook C1) |
| `payback`, `roi`, `lifecycleCost`, `costPerPart` | customer economics (payback: Handbook C6; ROI undiscounted; life-cycle per spec §9) |
| `platformBreakEven`, `localizationPayback`, `learningCurve` | platform and localization decisions (Handbook C8, C9, C2) |
| `costOfOwnership` | semiconductor equipment COO (Semiconductor Handbook Part XXXVI) |
| `SCENARIO_PRESETS` / `applyScenario` | Base, Optimistic, Conservative, Import-heavy, Localization, Make-heavy, Buy-heavy, High-volume, Low-volume, Customer-specific — every factor is printed as an ASSUMED note |

Every result is a `CalcResult` (formula, inputs with units, assumptions, citation) and has a
WHY? button in the UI.

## 5. Where cost models come from

* **Templates** — the legacy platform's three templates (`data/cost/cost-templates.json`, DEMO).
* **Configuration → BOM → cost** — `services/bomGen.ts`: the platform base price (ESTIMATE)
  and module list prices (ESTIMATE) become material lines; lines with UNKNOWN cost are listed
  as gaps, not priced. Standard content is never charged twice.
* **Inquiry package** — the same path, with a customer-supplied laser costed at 0.
* **Legacy import** — your own Cost Platform projects, into your browser only.
* **RFQ** — applying a quote selection rewrites BOM lines as `QUOTED` with supplier and lead time.

## 6. What the engine will not do

It does not invent rates, quotes, FX or margins. Rates in `data/cost/rates.json` are the legacy
seed values, labelled DEMO. Real rates belong in a private deployment (see SECURITY.md).
